Learning to leap in a new era of growth

Vision & Mission

Vision

To lead Southeast Asia in transferring modern, international-standard digital education solutions that fit Vietnam’s realities.

Mission

To make STEM – AI education universal, close the digital divide and nurture a generation of global citizens who shape their own future.

Our story

Founded in 2023, Learn to Leap (LtL) grew from a deep conviction: in the digital age, as Artificial Intelligence (AI) reshapes the world, technology education must never be a privilege for the few. We want every Vietnamese student — from big cities to disadvantaged regions — to have access to technology, so that no one is left behind.

To make that vision real, LtL set out to become a full-service STEM & AI education consultancy and solutions provider for K-12 schools. Every programme we deliver is carefully refined to align with the 2018 General Education Curriculum and the most advanced digital competency frameworks.

LtL chooses to be a close companion — from strategic advice and model transfer to hands-on delivery, standing beside schools as they unlock each student’s potential. We are also proud to be a strategic partner in connecting and delivering key national projects in STEM, AI and digital transformation in education.
