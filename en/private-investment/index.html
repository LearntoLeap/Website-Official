Private-investment STEM – AI programmes

When public funding isn’t ready, schools can still have a STEM – AI lab: a company invests in the equipment, and Learn to Leap transfers the curriculum and runs it together with the school.

Discuss the model

Three parties, one goal

Investing company

Funds the equipment and lab space under a plan approved by the school and education authority.

School

Provides the room and timetable; oversees quality and safety of the learning activities.

Learn to Leap

Transfers the curriculum, provides teachers or trains school staff, and runs competitions.

What each party gains

Schools

  • A standard STEM – AI lab without waiting for budget
  • Properly trained teaching staff
  • Higher education quality and school reputation

Parents & students

  • STEM – AI at school at a reasonable cost
  • Festivals and competitions at every level
  • Digital skills and technology thinking

Companies

  • Corporate social responsibility in education
  • A transparent model with regular reporting
  • A link to the local education ecosystem

Delivered transparently and by the rules

  1. 1

    Survey

    Assess the room and students’ needs.

  2. 2

    Propose

    Investment plan, curriculum and indicative fees.

  3. 3

    Approve & sign

    The school and authority review under current rules.

  4. 4

    Invest & install

    The company invests; Learn to Leap installs and transfers.

  5. 5

    Run & report

    Teaching, competitions and regular reports to the school.

* Fees, duration and the form of cooperation are agreed with each school, in line with local regulations.
