Stick 'Em — building with chopsticks

Stick 'Em is a STEAM learning kit from Singapore. Students join chopsticks with colourful connectors to build models, then add a controller board, sensors and motors so their models move and can be programmed. Learn to Leap is the partner bringing Stick 'Em to Vietnamese schools.

Chopsticks and connectors

Connectors at many angles (60°–360°), wheels, axles and rubber bands for building frames, bridges, cranes, cars and more from everyday chopsticks.

Programmable electronics

An ESP32 board with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and an LED matrix; works with ultrasonic, light, temperature–humidity, gyroscope and camera sensors plus servo motors.

Stick 'Em Dojo

Students compete with the models they build, on the path to the international Global Robotics Game.

Teacher training

Learn to Leap trains teachers to use Stick 'Em in STEM lessons, as at Bac Hong Primary School (November 2026).
