A complete, lean and effective roll-out

A transparent, coordinated 5-step process that strictly follows the Ministry of Education and Training’s standard frameworks.

  1. 1

    Survey

    Assess current conditions, needs & facilities.

  2. 2

    Advise & design

    Propose the right solution, curriculum & funding model.

  3. 3

    Install & transfer

    Deliver the Lab, equipment, learning materials & LMS.

  4. 4

    Train teachers

    TOT training so school staff can own the programme.

  5. 5

    Operate & support

    Co-teaching, running competitions & long-term support.

Core values in every partnership

Quality

International-standard programmes & equipment, localised to Ministry frameworks and measured by what students can actually do.

Long-term support

We stay after launch: updated learning materials, regular teacher development & ongoing technical support.

Local flexibility

Adapted to the facilities, budget & priorities of each school and each locality.
